I am a first-year PhD student in Computer Science at [Virginia Tech](https://cs.vt.edu/), advised by [Dr. Shaddi Hasan](https://shaddihasan.com/) and [Dr. Bo Ji](https://people.cs.vt.edu/boji/). My research investigates the need for cross-layer coordination between applications, cellular networks, and edge infrastructure to meet end-to-end SLOs in 5G systems.

Prior to Virginia Tech, I worked as a Researcher at [Fraunhofer Heinrich-Hertz-Institut](https://www.hhi.fraunhofer.de/) in Berlin, where I drove research within the [5G-COMPASS](https://5g-compass.de/) consortium (funded by BMDV). My work focused on MAC-layer resource allocation using supervised and reinforcement learning, and on joint WiFi/5G-ORAN integration using OpenAirInterface and FlexRIC.

I received my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (GPA: 1.47/88%, President's List) from [Constructor University](https://constructor.university/) (formerly Jacobs University Bremen), where I contributed to the [SupraCoNeX](https://supraconex.eu/) project on user-space WiFi rate adaptation. I have participated in [Google Summer of Code](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/) four times — twice as a contributor and twice as a mentor — working on WiFi rate and power control algorithms for [freifunk](https://freifunk.net/).
